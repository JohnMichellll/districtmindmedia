Standards

Editorial policy.

Independent coverage built around accuracy, attribution and a clear point of view.

What we cover

District Mind Media covers music, artists, releases, live shows and culture, with a particular interest in independent artists and the scenes shaping Colorado and beyond.

Reporting and sourcing

When a story relies on outside reporting, public listings or published information, we aim to identify the source. Live-news headlines are presented as source links rather than original District Mind reporting. Original commentary and editorial perspective are labeled as such.

Corrections

If we publish a material factual error, we will correct it. Readers can flag an issue through the Contact page.

Independence

District Mind Media is connected to the District Mind creative ecosystem, but editorial coverage should not be presented as an endorsement simply because an artist, release or company is connected to District Mind Records.

Updates

Pages may be updated as dates, lineups, releases and other facts change. Published and modified dates should be used as the guide to the current version.
