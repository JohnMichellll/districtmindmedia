About

Built from the same mindset.

District Mind Media is an independent music and culture publication connected to District Mind Records, built to document artists, releases, live rooms, and the ideas moving culture forward.

Our mission

We cover what moves culture before it becomes mainstream.

District Mind was built around a simple idea: a district is a network of people, and a mind is a shared way of thinking. Media is where that mindset becomes a record of what is happening now.

Our coverage centers on music discovery, artist profiles, release notes, live events, and culture. We especially follow independent artists and the work happening outside the traditional gatekeepers.
