THE PRINCIPLE

WE DON'T ERASE ARTISTS BECAUSE THEY STOP POSTING.

Some artists release every month. Some drop one song and disappear. Some use a distributor, some build a catalog themselves, and some eventually move on to something else.

District Mind can still give those artists a place in the ecosystem: a discoverable profile, links to their work, their story, their city, their catalog and the people who helped make it.

ACTIVE IS A STATUS. ARTIST IS AN IDENTITY.That distinction is the foundation of the Registry.
