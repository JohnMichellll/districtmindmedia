District Mind Media / Privacy

YOUR DATA. YOUR CHOICE.

District Mind Media is designed to collect only the information needed to operate the publication, respond to messages and build optional audience products.

What we collect

Depending on the feature you use, this may include information you voluntarily submit, such as an email address or message. Basic technical information may also be processed by hosting, security and analytics providers.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond to you, operate requested services, maintain site security, understand publication performance and improve District Mind Media. We do not publish private submissions without an editorial reason and appropriate basis.

Third parties

District Mind may use infrastructure and service providers for hosting, catalog lookup, news discovery, email and site operations. Specific providers can change as the publication grows.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact desk. This notice is an operational publication notice and may be updated as the company and its services evolve.
