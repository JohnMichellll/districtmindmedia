COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick answers

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Visitors can explore the public site. Permanent saved favorites are intended for Academy members, but authentication and cloud syncing are not live yet.

Why do favorites disappear?

Browser storage is not an account. Permanent favorites require member authentication plus database storage connected to that account.

Why is artwork missing?

Refresh once, search the full artist name, and open the exact release. If the image is still missing, record the artist, release title, page URL, and device. Do not replace a real cover with unrelated stock art.

Are playlists synced?

Current playlists save in this browser and can be exported, but do not automatically sync across devices.
