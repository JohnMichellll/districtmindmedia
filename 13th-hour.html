JOHN MICHELL ANNOUNCES 13TH HOUR, A PROJECT ROOTED IN THE MIDNIGHT MINDSET

District Mind Editorial Desk · Published October 14, 2026 · 3 min read

Some ideas come alive when the rest of the world goes quiet. For independent artist John Michell, that late-night mindset is at the center of 13TH HOUR, a new instrumental project now featured on his official release page.

The project grew from beatmaking, sampling and creative experimentation, with a personal story behind it: the album began as a birthday gift for Michell’s son. That origin gives the release a meaning beyond simply putting a collection of tracks online.

BUILT INDEPENDENTLY

Michell continues to develop his sound while building his independent music path through District Mind Records. Working across production and the wider creative process, he represents a familiar independent-artist approach: learning by doing, shaping ideas in real time and finding ways to get the work in front of listeners.

Instrumental projects put the focus on atmosphere, rhythm and the decisions that happen before a vocal ever enters the room. 13TH HOUR arrives as another marker in Michell’s ongoing creative catalog.
