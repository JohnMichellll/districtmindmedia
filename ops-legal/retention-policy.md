# Internal Records & Preservation Policy

Status: INTERNAL DRAFT — REVIEW WITH COUNSEL

## 1. Objective
Preserve reliable business and editorial records without collecting unnecessary sensitive information.

## 2. Normal records
Keep operational records long enough to support accounting, editorial accountability, corrections, rights questions and business history. Use documented retention periods and review them with counsel.

## 3. Legal hold
If the owner receives a lawsuit, subpoena, preservation demand, credible threat of litigation, or attorney instruction to preserve material:
1. Do not delete or alter potentially relevant records.
2. Preserve relevant Git history, published versions, source records, correspondence and decision logs.
3. Record when the hold began and what systems/data are covered.
4. Do not communicate legal conclusions through the newsroom workflow.
5. Contact qualified counsel.

## 4. Privacy minimization
Do not put passwords, API secrets, financial account credentials, government identifiers or unnecessary private-person data into these records.

## 5. Integrity
Records should be timestamped in UTC, identify the responsible workflow/person where appropriate, and preserve links, hashes or commit IDs when available.

This policy is an operational draft, not legal advice.
